# Skill/template friction

Found while authoring this Project. Each row: what happened, the evidence, and the check that would have caught it.

- **The joint Newton solve does not converge under hard clipping, and says nothing.** `dsp/src/circuit.rs`, `solve_joint`: 64 iterations with every step capped at 0.1 V, then the result is used whether or not it converged. With this Circuit at Drive 1 and a 1 V peak, 1 kHz input the step was still 4.9 V at the cap; the next samples ran away to 10^23 V and the WAV held NaN. Fixed here with SPICE's junction limiting (`pnjlim`) on each step and on the extrapolated starting point, a 200-iteration bound, and a debug assertion. Check: a test that drives each Model at 0 dBFS at its highest-gain Setting and asserts convergence and a finite output.
- **The PNP path had no test.** `Device::Bjt` with `polarity: -1.0` was only reachable through `Netlist::pnp`, which no Project used. Three tests added here. Check: one finite-difference Jacobian test and one operating-point test per device polarity.
- **576 kHz is not enough when a transistor leaves cutoff in under a microsecond.** `dsp/src/oversample.rs`: the documented reason for the rate is frequency warping. Here the gain and odd harmonics were right at 576 kHz, and H2 and H4 of a 5 kHz tone were 10 dB off, because the half-microsecond recovery delay was inside one step. Check: `references/diagnosing.md` could add a row, "even harmonics wrong only at high level and high frequency, odd harmonics right: time resolution of a switching edge; raise `MIN_CIRCUIT_RATE`".
- **`render` processed a mono file twice.** `render/src/main.rs` copied the mono channel into the right buffer and ran the Circuit on both. Every validation render is mono, so the Model side of validation cost twice what it needed. Fixed here (an empty right buffer); output is bit-identical. Check-less (a cost, not an error).
- **The harmonics Check has an absolute floor that rises above its relative floor at a muted Setting.** `harness/src/analog_harness/analysis.py` clamps amplitudes at -200 dBFS; `compare.py` ignores harmonics below -90 dBc. When the fundamental is below -110 dBFS (this Circuit at Drive 0 with a -40 dBFS tone: -115.6 dBFS) the clamp sits at -84 dBc, above the -90 dBc floor, so the Check compares numerical residue near -200 dBFS. It passed by hand here (both sides under the clamp). This may be the cause of green-overdrive's open failure at level 0; that was not tested. Check: compare the harmonic's absolute level with the clamp before counting it.
- **The scaffold makes a nested `.git`.** `scripts/new_project.py` runs `git init` in the new directory. Inside a repository that holds many Projects it has to be removed by hand. Check: skip `git init` when the target is already inside a work tree.
- **The scaffold lacks this repository's fixes.** Section reset (`model-sections`), the `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` export in both scripts, and the transient-operating-point guard in `spice.py` were copied in by hand from sibling Projects. Check-less until the Upstream Patches land.
- **"Do not run the bench" leaves the cost unmeasured at authoring time.** Only a wall-clock timing of `render` on a busy machine exists (about 860% of a core). Check-less.
